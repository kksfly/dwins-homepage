Welcome to INS Corp.

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to INS Corp., the premier South Korean privately-owned insurance and reinsurance brokerage firm serving clients since 1999.

Nominated as the exclusive partner of Assurex Global with over $28 billion in its annual premium volume and more than 600 partner offices in six continents, we provide a comprehensive risk management consulting, retail broking, reinsurance broking, and claim service for virtually every risk desired by the multinational corporations.

Since the foundation, we have achieved many extraordinary goals, and we have continued to challenge the status quo looking forward to upholding as the best company in its field, a company that is driven by its expertise.

Acting with respect, integrity, and pioneer spirit, we foster mutual growth with our valuable clients in line with our foundational philosophy of faith and trust.

The inevitability of risk, conjoined with its unpredictability makes it vital that corporations entrust us with decisions that require expertise and professionalism. This is where we demonstrate as an accredited partner.

We collaborate with publicly confident insurers and reinsurers around the globe to disperse risks and expand its solvency. We know how corporations can unlock potential through effective risk management. Our clients rely on us to create fascinating results out of risks. We transform risks into superior opportunity.

The ongoing success of INS Corp. is the result upon our clients’ trust which we endeavor to keep through the work of our professional team ready to create value for its clients by delivering enterprising performance.

I am pleased to sincerely thank you, our valued partners, and shareholders for your tremendous support in ensuring that we advance as the leading broker in South Korea.

I look forward to our successful partnership.

Thank you.

CEO Hans. K. Ryoo (유해강)
