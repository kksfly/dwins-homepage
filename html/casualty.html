Casualty & Specialty Solutions

Casualty insurance, often equated to liability insurance, is used to describe an area of insurance not directly concerned with life insurance, health insurance, or property insurance.

This category of insurance is quite broad, encompassing a range of situations and fields within the insurance industry. Strictly defining casualty insurance has become problematic in recent years with the rising popularity of multi-line insurance policies, but generally, it is designed for Third Party Liability Coverage of an individual or organization's negligent acts or omissions, Employee Theft and Dishonesty, and Burglary.

In the meantime, there are other types of casualty insurance developed in response to the latest trend in social and environmental issues: Cyber-Liability, Personal Information Leakage, Electronic Transactions, and more.

Our Casualty team has a wealth of experience in handling all forms of Casualty lines including specialist classes. We have built long-term and mutually beneficial relationships with many clients, providing both direct insurance and reinsurance broking services wherever and whenever needed.
