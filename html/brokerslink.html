Overview Assurex Global Verspieren Brokerslink

Global Broking Powerhouse

Brokerslink was created in 2004 as a global network of independent brokers. Since then, it has integrated best-in-class risk expert firms to become a strong alternative to the mainstream models in the insurance market.

Brokerslink was incorporated in Switzerland to become an integrated Global Broking Company with shared equity among members. This allows the organization to achieve potential growth and generate new revenue by executing global business strategies along with centralized service capabilities.

Its entrepreneurial spirit remains unchanged, keeping the organization lean to stay flexible and responsive to clients and business partners.

As a global insurance brokerage & risk management services organization, Brokerslink strives to provide clients with a superior experience in managing risks from beginning to end with solid industry expertise and in-house expert teams around the globe.

Partnership Benefits

• Swiss-incorporated Global Broking Company
• Shared equity among independent member firms
• Centralized global servicing & risk capabilities
• Lean, responsive and entrepreneurial
