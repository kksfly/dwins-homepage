Marine & Aviation Solutions

The origin of modern insurance comes from the marine insurance business and we take pride in our team’s expert ability to deliver results in this area. Through our extensive background in the marine cargo side we have developed an instinct for the nature of the various risks involved. This has allowed us to act as a catalyst to the mutual benefit between all parties involved in numerous occasions developing trust among our esteemed clientele.

Marine Cargo is our definite major business backed by our extensive experience in retail and reinsurance field, and especially project cargo is our specialized area.

We have worked on many projects in local and abroad, sometimes in collaboration with our engineering team which also has great experience and knowledge in various construction projects. This co-working allows us to provide the integrated and systematic service to our client.

Our team provides responsive, high security for our world-class services with competitive pricing.
