Engineering Risk Solutions

Our Engineering team is always ready for successful completion of your business.

Since the 1970's construction boom in the Middle East, many Korean construction companies have expanded their business opportunities across the globe based on their accumulated know-how over the years.

Moreover, construction today is characterized as mega structure, combined with high technology and diversified construction participants, requiring broad knowledge of related fields other than construction.

To meet market expectations, we offer comprehensive risk solutions from the initial stage to operation of construction projects both domestic and overseas. We also provide tailored advisory services and qualified information through our partnership with top global networks to major construction companies for their various needs.

We work with contractors, principals, lenders and insurance companies in respect of projects involving Buildings, Power Plants, Steel Plants, Petrochemical Complexes, Refinery Complexes, Roads, Tunnels, Railways, Bridges, Dams, Ports, Breakwaters, and other infrastructure projects.
