Property & Treaty Solutions

To prevent an uncertain physical loss of your company, business and or family properties caused by a physical accident such as fire, theft and some natural hazard, Property Insurance provides you the minimizing economical risk exposures and a chance of continuity of business and/or family affairs.

INS Corp.’s Property & Treaty Team work tirelessly on our clients’ behalf to negotiate the optimum insurance/reinsurance coverage at the most competitive terms available, and where standard products don’t fit the bill we work with our clients and markets to find innovative solutions that do.

Our team is ready to devote ourselves for your business on Property Facultative and Treaty. For the Property Facultative Risks, our technical expertise and experience allows us to ensure that the results we achieve for our clients are relevant, proper and delivered at the correct price and the best security available.

And for the Treaty Business, we work closely together with the international partners throughout the world to maximize the capacity and coverage to ensure that our clients can manage the safe retention strategy and risk portfolios.
